How to read the numbers

Proj
Sleeper's projection for this week under this league's scoring.
xPts
What this season's workload (carries, targets, red-zone work) normally produces, based on 2024 and 2025 games.
Avg
Actual points per game this season.
Trend
Avg minus xPts. Well above means scoring more than the workload supports; well below means the points should come.
Start
This week's expectation: mostly Proj, tempered by xPts and Avg.
Value
Rest-of-season weekly expectation: the next four weeks of projections, xPts and Avg.
